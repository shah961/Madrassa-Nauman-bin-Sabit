Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

NBS Islamic School System ("NBS", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the NBS website and its associated website features. It does not automatically cover information collected through separate offline processes, school records, admissions procedures, or other services unless those processes specifically refer to this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with the website.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact NBS through a website contact form or another communication method provided on the website, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Subject or reason for contacting us
  • The contents of your message
  • Any other information you choose to include in your communication

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive or confidential information through general website forms.

3. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When you visit a website, standard technical information may be processed by the website's hosting, security, or infrastructure providers. Depending on the services being used, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device or operating-system information
  • Pages requested or visited
  • Date and time of requests
  • Basic technical and security logs

Such information may be processed for purposes including website operation, security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and maintaining reliable website performance.

4. How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and messages
  • Providing information about NBS and its educational services
  • Communicating with visitors when necessary to respond to their enquiry
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the website
  • Protecting the website against misuse, spam, fraud, and security threats
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not intend to use information submitted through ordinary enquiries for unrelated purposes.

5. Contact Forms and Communications

If a contact form is available on the website, information submitted through that form may be transmitted to the service or system used to process the enquiry.

Information submitted through a contact form should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the enquiry. Visitors should not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through a general contact form.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use technologies that are necessary for basic website functionality, security, or performance.

If optional analytics, advertising, personalization, or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated where appropriate to describe those technologies and their purposes.

Your browser may also provide controls for managing or blocking cookies and similar technologies. Blocking certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

7. Analytics

NBS may introduce website analytics in the future to understand general website usage and improve the visitor experience. If an analytics service is enabled, the relevant service and its applicable privacy practices should be identified here and this policy may be updated accordingly.

We do not claim that a specific analytics service is currently active unless it has actually been configured on the website.

8. Third-Party Services

Some website resources may be provided by third-party services. For example, the website may use external content-delivery or font services to provide resources required for the site's appearance and functionality.

When your browser requests a resource from a third-party provider, that provider may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address and browser information.

Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. NBS does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

9. Google Fonts

This website may use fonts provided through Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's servers to retrieve the required font resources.

Because this involves a connection to a third-party service, certain technical information may be processed by that provider in accordance with its own policies.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the website and the information involved to help protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should therefore avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary website communications.

11. Data Retention

Information submitted through website enquiries should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, protect the website, or meet applicable legal or administrative requirements.

The actual retention period may depend on the system or service used to receive and store the information.

12. Data Sharing and Disclosure

NBS does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process communications, maintain security, or provide requested functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or a legitimate request from an authorized authority.

13. Children's Privacy

NBS is an educational institution serving students, including individuals who may be under the age of 18. The website is primarily intended to provide information about NBS and its educational activities rather than to independently create detailed online profiles of children.

Parents, guardians, and students should provide only information that is reasonably necessary when communicating with NBS through the website.

Where information concerning a child is submitted through the website, parents or guardians are encouraged to ensure that the information provided is appropriate and necessary for the purpose of the enquiry.

14. External Links

The NBS website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services. These websites are operated independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

NBS is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

15. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances in which your information was collected, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

You may contact NBS to ask about personal information you have voluntarily submitted through the website and, where appropriate, request correction or deletion of such information.

Requests can be made through the contact details provided on our Contact page.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled through the NBS website, please contact us using the information available on our Contact page.
