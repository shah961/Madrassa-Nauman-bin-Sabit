Frequently asked questions

What educational services does NBS provide?

NBS offers Quranic education, Islamic studies, Arabic language learning, modern academic support, character and personal development, extra-curricular programs, and family and community engagement.

Does NBS provide Quranic education?

Yes. This includes Hifz (memorization) with Tajweed, Tafseer, recitation training, and advanced Quranic studies.

What Islamic studies are offered?

Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, Aqeedah, and comparative religion studies.

Is Arabic language learning available?

Yes — grammar (Nahw and Sarf), reading and writing, classical literature, and conversational Arabic.

Does NBS provide academic support?

Yes. Mathematics, science, English, study skills and exam guidance are offered alongside religious education.

What personal development programs are available?

Morality and ethics sessions, leadership and teamwork training, time management and goal-setting workshops, and mentorship programs.

Are extracurricular programs offered?

Yes, including public speaking, Quran recitation competitions, art and calligraphy, community service, and seasonal camps.

Does NBS involve parents and the community?

Yes, through parent-teacher sessions, community lectures and seminars, and inter-school events.
