Education that grows the whole student

Every program at NBS is built around four things working together: the Quran, Islamic knowledge, the Arabic language, and a strong academic foundation.

Quranic Education

Hifz, Tajweed, Tafseer and recitation training, from first steps through advanced Quranic study.

Islamic Studies

Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah and Aqeedah, taught alongside comparative religion studies.

Arabic Language

Nahw, Sarf, reading, writing and classical literature, plus conversational Arabic.

Academic Support

Mathematics, science, English and study skills, integrated alongside religious education.
